About Global News Shorts

Global News Shorts is USA & world news, in shorts. The important stuff, daily — short clips from the hearings, speeches and moments that matter, cut straight to the point.

What you'll find here

  • Short vertical video clips, each with a full transcript you can read or search.
  • Clips are chosen for substance — what was actually said, not the spin around it.
  • New clips are added as they're published on our Instagram.

Transcripts

Every clip page carries a full transcript auto-generated by AI. It's there so you can search the words and skim the substance — but AI can mishear, so treat the video itself as the source of truth.
